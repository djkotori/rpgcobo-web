Let's set up battles with the enemies that appear on the map.

If you click "Encounters settings" from "Edit" in the title menu, or click the "Encounters settings" button in the selection tool panel, the "Encounters settings" dialog appears.

<div class="sspart">
<SS src='docs/battle-1.png' alt=''/>

##### <M part="A"/> Encounter group list
Add enemies that appear on this map in groups.

##### <M part="B"/> Properties
Edit battle-specific settings such as encounter rate, whether escape is possible, and BGM.

##### <M part="C"/> Enemy list
Place the enemies that appear in this battle.

##### <M part="D"/> Encounter range
You can paint and specify the appearance area of this encounter group.

##### <M part="E"/> Encounter conditions
If conditions are specified, encounters happen only when the conditions are met.
</div>

### <M code="f10d"/> Tips for grouping encounters
- To add randomness to enemy combinations, keep it to about 2 to 3 groups.
- On large field maps, separate enemy groups by encounter range.
- For bosses and fixed enemies, set encounter rate to 0 so they do not appear in random encounters.

### <M code="f10d"/> Battle test
You can actually test battles against the enemy group with the "<M code="e1c4"/> Battle test" button at the lower left of the dialog.
You can choose a save slot, open the menu, change equipment, and repeatedly test battles.
