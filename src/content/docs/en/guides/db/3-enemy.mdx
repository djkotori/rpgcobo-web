### Battle AI definitions
Battle AI is the behavior pattern settings used during battle.
Most weak enemies only need a simple attack-only AI, so they are not much work, but important boss characters can also be configured in elaborate ways, such as frequently changing attack patterns depending on the situation.

To edit battle AI, select "Battle AI" in the tab at the top right of the enemy editor. Once selected, the screen will look like the image below.

<div class="sspart">
<SS src='docs/enemy-2.png' alt='' minscale={0.7}/>

##### <M part="A"/> AI page list
You can add, delete, and move pages.

##### <M part="B"/> AI page properties
- Condition 1, 2: Conditions under which this page is selected
- Priority: Priority when this page is selected
- Continuous turns: How many turns this AI page continues after being selected
- Number of actions: How many commands are used per turn
- AI level: The intelligence level used when selecting commands, in five stages

##### <M part="C"/> Command list
The command list specifies the skills to use, the conditions for their use, and what kind of target to select.

</div>


### How to use multiple battle AI pages

Battle AI can define a complex behavior pattern even with a single page, but combining multiple pages allows you to create even more elaborate and distinctive AI.

Use pages to create AI with multiple modes such as the following.

- Enters a berserk mode when HP decreases
- Switches to a mode that prioritizes healing when an ally is in danger
- Randomly changes behavior patterns to confuse the player's strategy
- Taunts and acts careless when the opponent is weak
- Starts a countdown when HP decreases and self-destructs at 0
