The series of guides so far has explained how to make an RPG.  
It will still take time before the game is finished, but here we will explain how to distribute it so that everyone can play it at least once.

By "building" the project, you can package it into distributable files. **To build, you need to register as a user.**

### <M code="f10d"/> Register as a user

When you register as a user, you are first granted a license called the "Free license."  
The Free license has restrictions on the number of builds and target operating systems, but you can distribute your game for free with no cost.

<Aside>
If you want to use RPG-Cobo more deeply, purchase a license.
See here => [Pricing](/pricing)
</Aside>


Let's register as a user.

<Steps>
1. Click the second item from the bottom in the activity bar, "<M code="f03e"/> Account," and choose "<M code="ea77"/> Log in to RPG-Cobo."
A dialog like the following appears, and then a web browser opens automatically. <SS src='docs/register-1.png' alt=''/>

2. RPG-Cobo supports social login with "Google ID," "Github ID," and "Discord ID." Sign in with the ID you use. <SS src='docs/register-2.png' alt=''/>

3. Agree to the terms of use and complete "User registration."  

4. When browser login is complete, the app will also log in automatically, and the login process is finished. <SS src='docs/register-3.png' alt=''/>
</Steps>


### <M code="f10d"/> Account menu

After logging in, click "<M code="e853"/> Account" to show the account menu.  
Here you can check the account status, open the "[RPG-Cobo user site](https://user.rpg-cobo.com)", or log out.

<SS src='docs/register-4.png' alt=''/>
