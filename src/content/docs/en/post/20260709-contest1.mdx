## Total prize money: 1 million yen! Winning entries will be exhibited at Tokyo Game Show!

#### RPG-Cobo has been confirmed for exhibition at Tokyo Game Show 2026!  
And to take this opportunity to share user works with the world, **we are holding the first contest!**  
Winning entries will be playable by visitors at the RPG-Cobo booth at TGS. **This is your chance for your work to be seen by game fans around the world!**

We look forward to your enthusiastic participation!

### <M code="e65f"/> Submission requirements

- The work must be created with RPG-Cobo
- It must not infringe third-party copyrights, and must not violate public order and morals
- Completion level does not matter (short works and demos are accepted)
- Game export is not required
- Individual and team entries are both accepted
- One work per person, but resubmission during the period is allowed

### <M code="e65f"/> How to submit
---
- You can submit directly from the RPG-Cobo tool.  
  An icon of "<M code="ea23" c="#ff3"/>" appears at the bottom left of the tool, so click it to open the submission form.  
  - *RPG-Cobo user registration is required.*
  - *Tool version v0.1.8 or later is required. If the version is too old, the "<M code="ea23" c="#ff3"/>" icon will not appear.*
  <IMG src="post/20260709-2.png" alt=""/>

### <M code="e65f"/> About the Tokyo Game Show exhibit
---
- Winning entries (Grand Prize, Excellence Awards, Special Awards) will be exhibited as one of the contents inside RPG-Cobo at TGS2026
- Work introduction panels and author comments will be displayed (if desired)
- During the TGS period, we will actively introduce the works on the official website and official X account

### <M code="e65f"/> A request
---
- During the contest period, **please share your work progress and creation process on X and other social media.**  
  **We will actively repost and introduce the works from our official account as well.**
- **RPG-Cobo is currently in beta testing**, so there may be bugs, issues, or unfinished features in the tool and exported games.  
  Bug reports are accepted on the [official RPG-Cobo Discord server](https://discord.gg/mxKf6Qu7Hm). We ask for your cooperation with the beta test.
- The organizers may contact you directly by email regarding the submitted work. If so, we would appreciate your cooperation if possible.
- Copyright for submitted works remains with the entrant, but by submitting you agree that we may use the work for publicity activities including publication on RPG-Cobo and use of video materials. Please understand this in advance.

RPG-Cobo will continue its efforts to share user works with the world, starting with this Tokyo Game Show exhibit and the contest.
