RPG development tool RPG-Cobo began beta testing on June 5, 2026.  
During this beta test, we will focus on intensive debugging and improvements based on feedback and requests from users.
This is an important period for making RPG-Cobo "the best tool for creating a world-scale RPG experience," so we would greatly appreciate your support.


***See here for RPG-Cobo =>*** [Official RPG-Cobo website](https://rpg-cobo.com)

### <M code="e65f"/> Period
---
**June 5, 2026 to around August 2026 (until early access begins)**

### <M code="e65f"/> Participation requirements
---
**Anyone is welcome to participate freely.**
If you are interested in making RPGs, want to try the tool, or are willing to help improve it, we warmly welcome you.

### <M code="e65f"/> How to join
---
- Download here: [RPG-Cobo Download](https://rpg-cobo.com/download)
- If you are willing to help with feedback to the developers, such as bug reports or feature requests, please join the official RPG-Cobo Discord server (optional): [RPG-Cobo Official Discord](https://discord.gg/mxKf6Qu7Hm)
- Please share reports, opinions, and impressions in the various Discord channels. Content shared on X (formerly Twitter), blogs, and elsewhere will also be used as reference.

### <M code="e65f"/> What we would like you to help with during the beta test
---
Your feedback will help RPG-Cobo grow into a better tool. We especially welcome feedback such as the following.  

- **Honest impressions and opinions after actually using it**  
  Tell us what you thought of it as an RPG tool, including the good points and the things that felt questionable.
- **Bug and issue reports**  
It is very helpful if you include reproduction steps and the situation.
- **Requests and improvement ideas for features and usability**  
Specific proposals such as "this would be easier to use if it worked like this" are especially appreciated.
- **Opinions on the documentation**  
Please let us know about unclear parts or places where the explanation is insufficient.
- **Participation in surveys and online meetings**  
If we hold them, we would be happy if you could cooperate.

### <M code="e65f"/> Notes
---
- **Especially soon after the start of the beta test, there will likely be various bugs, issues, and unfinished features in the tool and in exported games. We will do our best to respond quickly, but please continue to support RPG-Cobo with patience.**
- During the beta test period, major bug fixes and feature improvements may cause projects under development to fail to open in newer versions. Please understand this in advance.
- For detailed investigation of reports, the development team may contact you individually. If so, we would appreciate your cooperation if possible.

### <M code="e65f"/> Contest planning underway
---
We are currently planning a **prize-backed contest for submitted works** for beta test participants.
Details will be announced later, so please look forward to it.

## We look forward to your participation in the beta test

RPG-Cobo is an open RPG development tool that grows together with all of you. Your opinions will help shape the future RPG creation experience.  
Please feel free to join and enjoy watching RPG-Cobo grow together with us.
