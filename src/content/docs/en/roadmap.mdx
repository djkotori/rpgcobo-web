## Autumn 2026: Early access and beyond

With early access, paid licenses will become available.  
Alongside this, we will focus on improving export functionality for each OS, building out a tool environment for developing more advanced 3D RPGs.

Early access is targeted to begin in October or November 2026, after thoroughly incorporating feedback from the beta test.  
The transition from beta test to early access will be seamless, allowing users to experience new features without interruption.

## Within 2026: Official launch in Japan and beyond

The official launch will take place once the features implemented up through early access are judged to be sufficiently stable and performant.  
In addition, a global service launch is planned for the first half of 2027.

Our goal is to continue providing an even more usable and advanced 3D RPG creation environment.

## We look forward to your feedback and requests about the roadmap!

RPG-Cobo's development is guided by feedback from our users.  
If you have any thoughts, requests, or feature suggestions regarding this roadmap, please feel free to share them with us.  
Your feedback has a major impact on the future development of RPG-Cobo.

Please share your feedback and requests through the #feature-request-ja/en channel on [Discord](https://discord.gg/mxKf6Qu7Hm),
the [GitHub Issues page](https://github.com/djkotori/rpgcobo-tool/issues),
or via the hashtag #RPGCobo on [X @RPG-Cobo official](https://x.com/RPG_Cobo).
