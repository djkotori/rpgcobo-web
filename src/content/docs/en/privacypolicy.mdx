Super Shark Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "the Company") establishes the following privacy policy regarding the handling of customers' personal information in the services provided on RPG-Cobo (hereinafter referred to as "the Service").

By choosing to use the Service, you are deemed to have consented to the collection and use of information related to this policy. The personal information we collect is used to provide and improve the Service.
We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy.

### Collection and storage of information
The Service stores the following information as personal information in its database and manages it securely. We do not collect or store any information not included in this list, such as credit card information.

- Social account information at the time of user registration: account ID, email address, icon, and information provided by the social provider
- Device information when using the tool: IP address, OS, CPU, GPU, installed memory, and device information deemed useful for troubleshooting tool issues and improving the service
- Web browsing history and usage history on the Service
- Payment information provided by the payment system when purchasing products on the Service

### Provision of personal information to third parties
1. Except in the following cases, we will not provide personal information to third parties without prior user consent, except as permitted by the Act on the Protection of Personal Information and other laws and regulations.
	- When a formal disclosure request based on law is received from a public authority such as a court or the police
	- When a user's actions on this website violate public order or morals and disclosure is deemed necessary to protect our rights, property, or services
	- When there is an imminent danger to a person's life, body, property, or similar interests and an urgent need exists
	- When a credit card company or other payment company makes an inquiry for the purpose of investigating fraudulent use
2. Notwithstanding the provisions of the preceding paragraph, in the following cases, personal-related information may be provided to specific third parties based on the user's consent (consent to this Privacy Policy).
	- For promotional activities to promote our services
	- For outsourcing part of our service operations
	- For analyzing how our services are used
